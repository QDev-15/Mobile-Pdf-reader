Overview

PDF Reader is a local PDF viewing and editing app. It opens, annotates, signs, exports, and manages PDF files you choose on your own device. Your PDF files and their contents are never uploaded to any server we operate — all file processing, including on-device text recognition (OCR), happens directly on your phone.

This policy explains what little data the app and the third-party services it relies on (advertising and billing) can access, and why.

Information we collect directly

We do not require an account, login, or sign-up to use PDF Reader. We do not operate our own servers that receive your files, your usage activity, or any personal information you type into the app.

Everything you create or import — opened PDFs, page edits, signatures, recent-file history — is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data, and we have no copy of it.

Information collected by third-party services

PDF Reader uses a small number of third-party SDKs to show ads and process the optional Pro purchase. These services may collect data under their own privacy policies, separate from ours:

On-device text recognition (OCR)

PDF Reader's text-recognition feature uses Google's ML Kit on-device text recognition model. It runs locally on your phone; page images are not sent to Google or to us for this feature to work.

Permissions the app requests

Internet access and network state — used only to request ads, process Play Billing purchases, and check for app updates. The app does not need, and does not request, access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or precise location.

Children's privacy

PDF Reader is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising shown in the app follows the ad-content settings available through Google Play.

Data retention and deletion

Because your files and app data stay on your device, you control retention directly: uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, removes everything the app has stored. There is no account or server-side copy for us to delete on your behalf.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same page with a new effective date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the revised policy.
